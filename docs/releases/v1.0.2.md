A visual update: app tiles in a premium dark style, plus three Home fixes. Install over 1.0.1; your settings and data are kept.

## New
- **Dark app tiles.** On Home, in Apps and in Search, each app's logo sits on a charcoal tile in the style of dark-mode icons, with a light focus ring. Apps whose artwork can't be adapted cleanly keep their own. New and updated apps get their tile automatically.
- **Settings > App icons.** Choose Dark or Light (each app's original artwork). Switching is instant.

## Bug fixes
- **Top row:** after Back or Home, moving down again starts at the first app.
- **Home rows:** returning to the rows under the top row starts again at the first row and title.
- **Hold OK:** holding OK on a title opens its menu once, without also choosing the menu's first option.
- **Top row:** the Play button stays visible while moving between titles.

## Install or update
- **Updating from 1.0.1 or 1.0.0:** download `epple-tv-1.0.2.apk` and install it over the existing app. Don't uninstall first. The first start prepares every app tile once in the background (a few seconds).
- **New install:** see [Installation](https://github.com/muhibtasam/epple-tv-launcher#installation).
