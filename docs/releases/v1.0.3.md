A Home update: the dock magnifies as you move along it, and a quiet line under the clock. Install over 1.0.2; your settings and data are kept.

## New
- **Dock magnification.** Moving along the dock, the focused app and its neighbours grow in a smooth wave that follows you, rising from the dock's baseline like the macOS Dock. The glass widens with the row so every icon stays on it, and with more apps than fit, the next one peeks past the glass instead of slipping under it.
- **Glance Line.** One short line under the clock: offline, sleep timer, Wi-Fi restored or unread notifications when they matter, otherwise the date (for example "Wed, 18 November"). It hides while Control Center or a notification is open.
